IKEA

Part of IKEA's site-wide UX/UI update focused on Skapa, a component and token-based page-building system. It empowers local markets to construct optimized layouts through a structured drag-and-drop workflow.

Client IKEA
Role Product Designer
Scope Design system / UX
Context System / Editorial / Product
Skapa Component System

The Skapa design system prioritizes modularity. Standardized UI blocks and tokens enable teams to rapidly assemble consistent, high-performing layouts while strictly adhering to IKEA's global visual identity.

Product Highlighting

Interactive room settings bridge the gap between inspiration and commerce. Distinct product hotspots overlaid on lifestyle imagery reveal clear pricing and add-to-cart actions, driving a seamless transition into the purchasing flow.

Dynamic Content

Product pages are engineered for fluid interactions. Changing product variants—like a chair's color—triggers seamless dynamic content injection, updating media galleries instantly without page reloads or layout shifts.

Contextual Shopping

A seamless 'Shop the Look' experience integrated directly into the mobile interface. Bottom sheet components provide immediate access to product details and pricing without losing the context of the inspirational imagery.

Onboarding & Permissions

Build trust through transparent onboarding. Complex data permissions are presented with clear, friendly illustrations and straightforward language, ensuring users feel entirely in control of their privacy.
