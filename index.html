The Shift

AI doesn't just speed up design—it speeds up thinking. It turns an early hunch into options, pressure-tests direction, and generates UX/UI, copy, prototypes, and code in minutes. That doesn't make designers less valuable—it changes what "design" is.

The New Job

The job now is making quality repeatable: setting direction, defining the standards, and architecting the systems that execute them. Component libraries, agentic guards, and strict behavioral rules keep the output coherent—even when execution happens at lightspeed.

The Advantage

When making gets cheaper, judgment becomes the advantage. The winners won't be the teams who produce the most screens—they'll be the teams who align faster, decide clearer, and ship consistent experiences at scale.

The Operating Model

I'm building for: AI speed, human taste—backed by systems teams can actually ship. Not speed for speed's sake, but momentum with quality. Create clarity on what "good" looks like, and teams ship with confidence—again and again.
