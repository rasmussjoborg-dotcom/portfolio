Qapital

Qapital is a comprehensive money management app built on behavioral economics. It empowers users to take control of their finances by automating saving, investing, and budgeting. By connecting daily habits to long-term financial goals, the platform transforms abstract financial concepts into actionable, everyday behaviors.

Client Qapital
Role Product Designer
Scope Product design / UX
Context Fintech / Personal Finance
Payday Divvy

The Funding tab gives users full control over how each paycheck is split. A visual bar breaks down spending vs. saving allocations across goals like Emergency Fund and Down Payment—turning abstract budgeting into a tangible, adjustable interface.

In Context

The goal picker surfaces personalized templates—New Ride, Emergency Fund, Vacation—organized by relevance. Users start saving in under 30 seconds by tapping a visual card that resonates with their ambition.

01 — First Impressions

The onboarding sequence leads with three distinct value propositions—goal-based saving, automated investing, and shared finances—each on a color-coded screen. This immediately communicates breadth while keeping the activation CTA persistent and prominent.

02 — Goal Definition

The experience is sliced into digestible steps: a narrative introduction, intent categorization ("Do something", "Buy something", "Pay off debt"), and a visually rich template gallery. Design callouts highlight how each screen reduces cognitive load while maintaining forward momentum.

03 — Rules & Automation

After defining a goal, users set a target amount and achievement date, then select automated saving rules—52 Week Rule, Payday percentage—that turn passive intent into active, recurring transfers. The progress stepper provides constant verification of where users are in the setup.

04 — Account Linking

The critical friction point of connecting a funding source is handled through Plaid integration. The flow moves from a clear prompt ("Next, link a checking account") through bank selection to an immediate confirmation state—"Great, your checking account is connected"—with the goal card visible throughout for continuity.

05 — Personalization & Plan

Post-activation, a narrative overlay transitions users into deeper personalization: "What's important to you?", partner savings options, and a tiered subscription picker (Basic, Complete, Premier). Each step uses progress indicators and playful illustrations to maintain engagement through monetization.

Weekly Insights

Push-style insight cards celebrate progress: "Your Qapital balance increased by $125." Paired with 3D cash illustrations and goal progress bars, these moments reinforce positive behavior and keep users returning to check their momentum.

Saving Rules

Qapital's signature Rules—Payday, Set & Forget, Guilty Pleasure—each use distinctive icons and plain-language descriptions. The card-based layout makes it effortless to browse and stack multiple rules, turning the savings engine into something playful and personal.

Goal Selection

Highly visual goal templates—each tagged as Popular or Recommended—use aspirational imagery to connect financial planning with emotional motivation. The interface transforms abstract dollar amounts into concrete, desirable outcomes.

Qapital in the Wild

The product lives at the intersection of everyday moments and financial intention. A café table, a Visa card, and the "Build your goal list" screen—saving starts wherever the user happens to be.
