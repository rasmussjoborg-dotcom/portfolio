IKEA

IKEA / Product Designer / Bygga system

Worked on IKEA's large-scale UX/UI refresh through Bygga, a drag-and-drop component and token system that helped local markets create pages faster while keeping the experience structured, usable, and consistent.

Scope Design system / UX
Context System / Editorial / Product
Impact Faster, more consistent local page creation
Challenge

IKEA needed a shared system that could support a global UX/UI shift while still giving each market the flexibility to create pages for local needs.

Approach

I worked on the Bygga system itself, shaping components and patterns that made page creation simpler in practice while preserving structure, clarity, and brand consistency.

Outcome

The result was a more usable foundation for editorial and product pages across markets: easier to work with, faster to assemble, and more consistent by design.

Qapital

Qapital / Product Designer / US retirement feature

Worked on a retirement-focused feature for the US market inside Qapital's automated money app, turning a legally and financially complex space into an experience that felt simpler, clearer, and trustworthy enough to act on.

Scope Product design / UX
Context Fintech / Investing / Retirement
Impact Lowered the barrier to starting retirement saving in-app
Challenge

Design around the complexity of IRA structures, taxes, and state-specific rules while also answering a bigger product question: how could Qapital earn enough trust to be considered part of a user's retirement plan?

Approach

Built the flow around Qapital's core behavior model: users chose how to save through rules, selected a risk profile, and then set a retirement goal. The design focus was to keep each step simple, transparent, and easy to trust.

Outcome

The feature extended Qapital's savings and investing ecosystem into longer-term planning, with success defined less by becoming a user's only retirement partner and more by helping them start saving meaningfully, even at a 10% level.
