Filippa K

Led the full UX and UI for Filippa K's e-commerce site, translating a brand built on Scandinavian minimalism, timeless wardrobe staples, and mindful consumption into a premium digital shopping experience.

Client Filippa K
Role Product Designer
Scope E-commerce / UI / UX
Context Fashion / DTC / Scandinavian minimalism
Homepage and brand commerce

The homepage balances brand storytelling with commerce. Large, full-bleed editorial imagery sets a premium tone, while restrained navigation and immediate access to key collections provide clear product paths.

Campaign & Editorial

Editorial lookbooks prioritize visual storytelling. The interface recedes into the background, allowing the photography and styling to take center stage through structured, varying grids.

Product listing and filters

The product listing page focuses on precision and clarity. A clean grid, subtle color variation indicators, and a minimalist filter row ensure that product discovery is seamless and intuitive.

Product detail

The shopping cart slides out smoothly over the product view, minimizing disruption. It presents a clear order summary, integrated cross-sells, and localized payment options for a frictionless path to purchase.

Checkout flow

The checkout experience maintains the site's quiet clarity. A structured two-column layout keeps the order summary visible while the user navigates a highly legible, distraction-free form.
